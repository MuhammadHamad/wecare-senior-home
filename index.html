Our home care services

In-home senior care, built around each person

From a few hours of help each week to steady daily support, our caregivers provide personalized non-medical home care that keeps seniors safe, comfortable, and independent at home.

Personal care

Respectful personal care assistance with bathing, dressing, grooming, and hygiene, always with privacy and dignity.

Companion care

Friendly companionship and emotional support, from conversation and activities to easing the loneliness of aging at home.

Respite care

Trusted respite care that gives family caregivers a chance to rest, work, or travel, knowing their loved one is in good hands.

Meals & housekeeping

Nutritious meal preparation and light housekeeping that keep the home clean, comfortable, and free of hazards.

Medication reminders

Gentle, reliable medication reminders so prescriptions are taken on time and exactly as directed.

Mobility & transportation

Steady mobility assistance around the home, plus transportation to medical appointments, errands, and groceries.

Common questions

Home care questions, answered

Helpful answers for families researching in-home senior care in Lawrenceville, Duluth, Suwanee, and Buford.

Is this medical or non-medical home care?
We provide non-medical in-home care: personal care, companionship, meal preparation, light housekeeping, medication reminders, mobility help, and transportation. We do not provide skilled nursing or medical treatment. If your loved one needs medical care, we’re glad to help you understand your options.
What areas do you serve?
We serve Lawrenceville, Duluth, Suwanee, Buford, and the surrounding Gwinnett County communities in northeast metro Atlanta. If your town is nearby and not listed, call (404) 317-4137 and we’ll let you know if we can help.
How much does in-home senior care cost?
Cost depends on how many hours of care your loved one needs and the type of support involved. We offer flexible daily and weekly options, and we’ll give you clear pricing during a free consultation, with no obligation.
Can you provide overnight or extended-hours care?
We build flexible schedules around each family’s needs. Call us to talk through extended-hours or overnight support and we’ll let you know what we can arrange based on availability.
How do families pay for home care?
Many families use private pay, while others may have long-term care insurance or veterans’ benefits that can help. We’re happy to walk you through the options during your consultation.
How quickly can care start?
In many cases we can begin within a few days of your first call. Reach out at (404) 317-4137 and we’ll move as quickly as your family needs.
How do you choose your caregivers?
We select caregivers for their compassion, reliability, and experience, and we match each one thoughtfully to the client and family they’ll support.
